# Ethics

Foundry will be judged by the worst PR it opens, not the best.

## We will not

- Farm `good-first-issue` across the firehose.
- Open PRs against a repo whose policy we have not parsed.
- Hide that a packet was agent-prepared.
- Forge DCO/CLA sign-off.
- Comment “LGTM” as a bot on our own PR.
- Retry a closed-as-unwanted patch with a different title.
- Use maintainer review time as free CI.

## We will

- Disclose.
- Prefer docs, types, icons, tests, and changelog hygiene over feature work.
- Park more than we ship.
- Treat “no” as permanent until they invite us back.
- Keep the scorecard honest — including the 2026 slop incidents that motivated this repo.

## Proof over doctrine

orca-fleet’s predecessor died of missions that were not run. Foundry inherits the same rule: a station that has not produced a SHA-bound evidence manifest is `doctrine-only`. The operator console labels v2 sandbox sessions `dry-run` until an E2B key harvests a real patch.
